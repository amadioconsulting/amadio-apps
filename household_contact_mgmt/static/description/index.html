Key Features

🏠

Household Records

A dedicated Household model that groups any number of contacts under one shared address, phone, and email. One record — entire family.

✍️

Auto-Generated Salutations

Automatically builds "John & Mary Smith" from member names. Override with any custom salutation — "The Smith Family", "Rev. & Mrs. Chahinian", etc.

👥

Member Roles

Assign roles: Primary Contact, Spouse/Partner, Child, Dependent, Other. Filter and report by role for targeted outreach or age-group ministry.

📬

Address Inheritance

When a contact is linked to a household, the address is automatically suggested from the household record — keeping all members in sync.

📅

Key Date Tracking

Store wedding anniversary, membership/registration date, individual birth dates, and baptism dates — all searchable and reportable.

🔍

Search Any Member

Find a household by searching for any member's first or last name — even if they're a child or secondary contact.

💬

Chatter & Activity Tracking

Full Odoo chatter on every household record — log notes, schedule follow-ups, and track changes with full audit history.

🧩

Extends Standard Contacts

No data migration. The Household field appears directly on existing res.partner records — link any existing contact to a household instantly.
