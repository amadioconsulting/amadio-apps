The Location Mistake That Costs You Hours
Odoo's default validation doesn't check whether products belong in the location they're being moved to. Your rules live in someone's head — not in the system.

⚠️ Misrouted Products

Cold-chain items land in ambient storage. Hazardous materials go to the wrong zone. Valuable stock ends up in a bulk bin. The system said nothing.

⚠️ Wasted Cycle Counts

Products placed in wrong locations create phantom inventory discrepancies. Hours spent counting stock that's simply in the wrong place.

⚠️ New Staff Errors

Warehouse rules exist in binders and verbal handoffs. New staff validate shipments confidently — and incorrectly — until someone notices weeks later.

⚠️ Compliance Exposure

For regulated products, wrong-location shipments can trigger audit findings, fines, or product recalls. Manual checks don't scale.

How It Works
Three steps from rule creation to real-time enforcement. No developer needed.
1

Configure Rules

Go to Inventory → Configuration → Location Rules. Set which product categories are restricted from which storage locations. Choose Warn or Block.

2

Automatic Enforcement

When a warehouse operator clicks Validate on a delivery or transfer, the module scans all move lines against your rules in real time.

3

Warn or Block

In Warn mode, the operator sees a clear violation list and can confirm or cancel. In Block mode, validation is rejected with a precise explanation.

Key Features
Built for warehouse managers who need rules that actually stick.
📋

Category-Based Rules

Apply rules at the product category level — one rule covers hundreds of SKUs. Rules cascade to child categories automatically.

🚦

Warn vs. Block Mode

Choose per rule: Warn shows a confirmation dialog letting managers override, Block is a hard stop that prevents validation entirely.

🔴

Picking Form Warning Banner

A prominent visual warning appears on the stock picking form as soon as a violation is detected — before the operator even clicks Validate.

📍

Any Transfer Type

Works on receipts, deliveries, and internal transfers. Configure rules differently per operation type using Odoo's existing operation codes.

✏️

Manager Notes on Rules

Attach a plain-language explanation to each rule — "Cold chain items only in Zone A" — so operators understand why the restriction exists.

🔒

Role-Based Access

Stock Managers configure rules. Stock Users see validation results only. No risk of accidental rule modification by floor staff.

Who Needs This Module

🧊 Cold Chain & Food Distribution

Prevent ambient-temperature products from being validated into refrigerated zones — and vice versa. Critical for food safety compliance.

⚗️ Chemical & Hazardous Materials

Enforce separation rules for incompatible chemical categories. Ensure dangerous goods never land in general-access areas during transfers.

📦 Multi-Client 3PL Warehouses

Restrict each client's inventory to their dedicated zones. Eliminate costly cross-client contamination errors automatically.

🏥 Pharma & Medical Devices

Maintain validated storage conditions required by GMP and regulatory standards. Route products to approved locations by default.

🏭 High-Value Manufacturing

Keep components in controlled-access areas and prevent expensive items from being moved to open picking zones incorrectly.

🛒 Omnichannel Retail

Prevent B2C fulfillment stock from being moved to B2B bulk areas during high-volume periods when shortcuts are tempting.
