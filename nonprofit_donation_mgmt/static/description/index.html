The Donation Management Gap in Odoo
Odoo's accounting module handles invoices and payments brilliantly. But donations aren't sales — they need receipts, campaigns, donor histories, and year-end statements that standard invoicing doesn't provide.

📋 No Dedicated Donation Record

Tracking donations as invoices or journal entries creates clutter, requires accounting knowledge, and produces receipts that don't meet Canada Revenue Agency or IRS receipt standards.

📄 Tax Receipts Are Manual Work

Generating proper charitable donation receipts — with receipt numbers, official language, donor information — requires manual document creation outside of Odoo. Prone to errors and delays.

📊 No Year-End Giving Statements

Donors expect a year-end summary of all their gifts. Without a dedicated system, staff spend January manually compiling Excel reports — or worse, emailing donors individually.

🎯 No Campaign or Fund Tracking

Building campaigns in Odoo's standard tools is a workaround, not a solution. There's no clean way to track how much a fundraising campaign raised toward its goal.

Key Features
💳

All Payment Methods

Track cash, cheque (with cheque number), e-transfer, credit card, and other methods. Cheque duplicate detection prevents double-entry errors.

📄

Tax Receipt PDF

Professional QWeb PDF receipts with sequential receipt numbers, donor details, amount in words, charity registration field, and authorized signature line.

🎯

Funds & Campaigns

Create donation funds (General Fund, Building Fund, Bursary) and fundraising campaigns with goals and date ranges. Track progress against targets in real time.

📬

Year-End Giving Statements

A built-in wizard generates a PDF giving statement for any donor for any year — showing all donations, receipt numbers, and totals. Print or email in seconds.

📊

Accounting Integration

Optional: post donations directly to accounting as journal entries. Link funds to GL accounts for clean financial reporting without double work.

🔢

Auto-Numbered Sequences

Donations and receipts are auto-numbered with configurable sequences (DON-2026-00001, RCP-2026-00001). Audit-ready from day one.

Organizations This Module Serves

⛪ Churches & Faith Communities

Sunday collections, memorial donations, building campaigns, special appeals — all tracked against the right fund, with receipts ready for parishioners at tax time.

🌱 Registered Charities

CRA-compliant (Canada) and IRS-ready (USA) receipts with charity registration number, sequential numbering, and all required fields for official charitable donation receipts.

🎓 Schools & Educational Foundations

Alumni giving programs, bursary funds, annual campaigns — manage multiple funds and campaigns simultaneously with full donor history and giving statement generation.

🏥 Healthcare Foundations

Equipment campaigns, patient care funds, and annual giving programs with proper receipting and donor relationship tracking built into Odoo.

🤝 Social Service Organizations

Non-profits running programs funded by grants and individual giving. Track individual donations separately from grant income with clean fund accounting.

🎭 Arts & Cultural Organizations

Membership organizations, heritage associations, and arts foundations that rely on individual donations as a core revenue stream need proper receipting built in.
