Frequently Asked Questions

Does this post journal entries or affect my accounting?
No — this module is a deposit preparation and documentation tool. It reads from posted payments but does not create or modify journal entries. Your accounting records remain entirely in Odoo's standard flow.
Can I add manual lines (e.g. cash collections not tied to a specific payment)?
Yes. Deposit lines can be entered manually with any payer name and amount — no Odoo payment record is required. This is ideal for counting cash from a collection box or petty-cash deposit.
What if I accidentally add the wrong payment?
While the deposit is in Draft or Prepared status, you can freely add or remove lines. Once confirmed (with a bank reference), the deposit locks to protect your audit trail.
Does this work outside Canada?
Absolutely. The bank info fields (institution, transit, account) are free-text — you can enter any bank's routing identifiers. The PDF layout works for any bank in any country.
Can different companies have different bank configurations?
Yes. Bank information is stored per company in Accounting → Configuration → Settings. Each company sees only its own deposits and bank details.
