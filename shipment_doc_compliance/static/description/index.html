How Pre-Shipment Document Compliance Works

Three enforcement layers create a seamless, non-bypassable compliance checkpoint at the moment of shipment validation.

Layer 1 - Visual Alert Banner

A prominent warning banner appears on every outgoing shipment form requiring document verification. Warehouse staff cannot miss it. It is the first thing they see when they open the transfer.

Layer 2 - Mandatory Acknowledgment Checkbox

The Validate button is blocked until staff explicitly check that all required compliance documents are included. No checkbox means no shipment. Simple, enforceable, and audit-ready.

Layer 3 - Automated Audit Notification

On validation, the system logs a chatter note and sends an email notification recording who confirmed compliance, on which shipment, and at what time. Full audit trail with zero manual effort.

Key Features and Configuration

Per Operation Type Control

Enable or disable compliance enforcement per operation type. Apply only to outgoing deliveries, customer returns, cross-docking, or any custom operation type you define.

Per Customer and Contact Rules

Flag specific customers requiring stricter document compliance, ideal for regulated industries, international customers, or high-value accounts.

Parent Company Inheritance

Compliance flags set on a parent company automatically cascade to all child contacts and delivery addresses. Configure once and apply everywhere.

Zero Training Required

Embedded directly into the standard Odoo stock picking interface. No new screens and no separate apps. Staff work exactly as always with compliance guardrails added.

Chatter and Email Audit Trail

Every compliance confirmation is logged in the shipment chatter and triggers an automated email. A defensible audit record for certifications, inspections, or disputes.

Lightweight and Non-Invasive

Only 148 lines of code. No schema overhaul. Installs in under two minutes alongside existing stock, sale, and mail configurations without modification.
